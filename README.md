# Homie

Homie is a rental management platform designed to improve the relationship between landlords and tenants.

## Problem

Rental communication and records are often scattered across text messages, emails, receipts, and documents. This can lead to:

* Missed or unclear communication
* Poor maintenance tracking
* Disputes without reliable records
* Confusion around rent and expenses
* Important documents being difficult to find
* Lack of a shared source of truth for both parties

## Solution

Homie brings the rental relationship into one shared platform.

It provides a centralized place for landlords and tenants to:

* Manage rental information
* Track rent and expenses
* Report and manage maintenance issues
* Store important documents
* Communicate with a clear record of conversations
* Keep an organized history of rental activity

The goal is simple: **make renting more transparent, organized, and manageable for both sides.**
